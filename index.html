The PIR® Gallery

The photo albums of Psychedelics In Recovery™ – PIR® — not tied to any one event. Conventions, gatherings, service years, whatever the fellowship makes next: every album lives here, served straight from the fellowship's repository, fast anywhere in the world, and costing nothing but care. Born from a WhatsApp request that went wonderfully rogue.

How this gallery began

It started with a simple favor: getting Anne R.'s Pixieset album onto gallery.psychedelicsinrecovery.org for the Inaugural Convention photos. One WhatsApp thread later, the fellowship had a shared Google Drive gathering everyone's photos from Denver — and a decision: the photos of our recovery deserve a home we own. So the fellowship built this gallery on GitHub: no platform, no expiry, no cost — the Pixieset album and Drive folder that sparked it are woven right in below.

Add your photos
